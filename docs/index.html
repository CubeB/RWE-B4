Orders and unit behaviour

  • Reclaim features and units, with progress shared between builders and resources credited as work is done
  • Repair, patrol (engages en route, single click patrols between two points) and capture orders, each with panel button and cursor
  • Self‑destruct countdown (Ctrl‑D, 5 s, cancellable) using the unit's SelfDestructAs weapon
  • Transports: load and unload orders with TA's own cursors and panel buttons; carried units ride the transport's QueryTransport piece and are set down on clear ground
  • Bombers fly through the target on their run, release when the timing is right, and turn back after a short run‑out or at the map edge instead of flying off the map
  • Ships and shipyards can be placed and built in water (a missing MaxWaterDepth now means any depth)
  • Units rock with recoil when they fire
  • Pathing: slope‑aware speed and routing, no corner squeezing between obstacles, unreachable destinations end at the closest reachable point
  • Builders no longer get stuck on unreachable build sites; nanoframes killed while unattended die cleanly

Economy and game flow

  • Spending throttles continuously to income when a stockpile runs dry; stalled resource bars flash red
  • Game‑over panel (victory / defeat / draw, game time, units destroyed and lost) instead of quitting; spectate afterwards
  • PAUSED uses TA's own title artwork from IGTITLES.GAF
  • F1 hotkey reference overlay; F10/F11 toggle the debug windows, Escape closes them and the keyboard is never trapped

Visibility

  • Line of sight, fog of war and radar in the deterministic sim; unseen enemies hidden, radar contacts as blips, unexplored features hidden
  • Sight is blocked by higher ground, so hills cast shadows
  • Fog drawn the way TA draws it: remembered ground in grey, unknown ground black, a 32‑unit boundary eroded by a few pixels of fixed noise (measured from the original)
  • Minimap only reveals what the player can see or detect

Effects

  • Construction: green pulse plus a wireframe of the camera‑facing edges that blends green → white → black; texture sweeps up over the frame as it completes; nano spray of small green squares from the builder's nozzle; shadow kept but cut out under the frame
  • Burning features: fire‑starting weapons ignite trees and foliage, fires spread and leave burnt remains, using each feature's own burn sequence
  • Building deaths run the COB Killed script: pieces fly off with smoke and fire trails and impact explosions
  • Reclaimed features play their golden dissolve sequence
  • Model lighting moved to match TA's low sun from the left, with a flatter curve and darker shadows; sight ignores the seabed under water

Skirmish AI

  • Perception through fog of war, threat map, scouting, expansion build order (solars, extractors on real patches, lab, radar, towers, metal makers), factories, constructors that assist
  • Single‑army rally, defend and attack cycle; Easy / Standard / Hard / Brutal profiles via --ai-difficulty
  • Debug panel and 30‑second status lines in rwe.log; verified live on Coast To Coast

Build and tooling

  • Windows checkouts without symlinks build (real asio include path); MinGW runtime DLLs copied next to the executables
  • Headless‑safe logging for tests; 34 new test cases across orders, pathing, economy, visibility and burning
